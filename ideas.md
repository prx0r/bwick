# ideas.md — product idea bank (2026-09-25)

Anything with a price and a buyer lives here until it graduates to products.md
or dies with a reason. Newest first.

## Fantasy-brick Christmas — PEOPLE (6)

1. **Winter wizard** — starry robe, glowing staff, snowy owl, ice-castle base.
   Flagship: magical, premium, birthday-safe (no Santa = December birthdays included).
2. **Nutcracker soldier** — uniform IS the product. Existing purchase habit,
   military-precision brick aesthetic, premium mantelpiece energy.
3. **Santa / Mrs. Claus self** — red suit, beard/braids, sack of minis, chimney
   base. The obvious one, done better via likeness.
4. **Frost knight** — ice-blue armor, snowflake shield, frozen steed accent.
   Fantasy-nerd gift buyer who finds Santa boring. Underserved.
5. **Ice queen** — flowing frozen gown (brick-dress challenge; capes drape fine),
   crystal base. Girls'/women's gift lane, huge.
6. **Yeti / gentle giant** — white fur texture, tiny Santa hat, holding a lantern.
   Cute + funny, kids love it.

## Fantasy-brick Christmas — PETS (6)

1. **Reindog** — antlers + glowing red nose + harness with bells. Highest
   scream-potential SKU in the catalogue. LEAD WITH THIS.
2. **Santa-pup** — red suit body, white beard ruff, hat. Classic, safe, sells
   to grandmas.
3. **Elf-cat** — pointy ears (already there!), green tunic, jingle collar.
   Cats finally get their lane.
4. **Penguin-pal** — tuxedo markings already penguin coloring on many dogs;
   lean in with a scarf. Minimal retexture, maximum cute.
5. **Gingerbread pup** — brown frosting palette, icing-squiggle details,
   candy-button base. Edible-looking without being edible (state that).
6. **Toy-soldier pup** — tall hat, drum, parade base. Pairs with human
   nutcracker as a set (sell the pair, always).
