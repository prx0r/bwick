# thisisit.md — three collections, one display format (2026-09-25)

Three collections, all using the **same personalised brick-figure display
format**, but targeting different gift interests.

| Collection | Identity | Themes |
|---|---|---|
| **BWIZARD** | Fantasy and adventure | Wizards, dragons, knights, princesses, elves, magical castles, enchanted forests |
| **BWITCH** | Esoteric, mystical and gothic | Tarot, astrology, witches, familiars, moon phases, alchemy, celestial symbolism |
| **BWICKMAS** | Christmas gifting | Santa, elves, festive pets, Christmas couples, family ornaments, snow scenes |

Everything else is shared: one figurine engine, base/mount/accessory swaps per
context, preview-before-print, QR on everything with a code.
