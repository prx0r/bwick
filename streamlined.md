# streamlined.md — brand retirement + remapped lines (2026-09-25)

## Collision alert (this is why we check)

- **BWICK is taken** — an existing creator-merch brand (YouTube, Twitch, TikTok,
  Discord, full social stack) with its own "Bwickmas Collection." Same name, same
  Christmas collection concept. Not a gray area — a doppelgänger.
- **BWITCH is taken** — Indian lingerie brand, retail chains, $18M revenue.
  Different category, same commerce airspace.

**Decision: retire all three names as brands.** Lines survive as neutral
descriptors — Roast line, Mystic line, Holiday line — ideally under **Figg**,
which came back clean. Better now than via takedown in December.

## What does NOT change

Same doctrine throughout: one figurine engine, base/mount/accessory swaps per
context, preview-before-print, QR on everything with a code. **The names changed;
nothing else had to.**

## Open question (flagged, not resolved)

Several SKUs appear in **both** Roast and Holiday: Santa self, Santa/elf kitty,
mistletoe-kiss pair, stocking-filler minis, nutcracker self. Two listings for
the same physical product splits reviews and confuses buyers. Decide once:
Holiday owns the Christmas SKUs during Q4 (they move into the Roast line in
January), **or** Roast owns them year-round and Holiday is only a shop section
that links to them. One owner per SKU.
