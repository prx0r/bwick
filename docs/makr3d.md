# Makr3D — UK 3D print fulfilment (imported 2026-09-25)

Run by Yorkshire3D Ltd, Huddersfield (trading 2019, 150K+ orders, 4.9/5,
company 15466679). Hundreds of Bambu printers, 500+ plates/day, 1–2 day dispatch.

## Commercial terms

- **No monthly fee, no MOQ.** Per-product fulfilment + shipping; £1 minimum per
  dropship order. Margin = your retail − fulfilment − postage − marketplace fees.
- Example live prices (ex-VAT, ex-postage; quote your exact file, ~20s, no account):
  - Benchy-class single-colour PLA: **US$1.75** (retail ~£3–10)
  - Fidget-class palm articulated PLA: **US$3.39** (retail ~£12–20)
  - Larger multi-part articulated: **US$10.30** (retail ~£25–35)
  - Multi-colour (AMS ≤4 colours) auto-quoted; complex purge waste hand-priced.
- Materials: PLA + PETG (resin planned). Faithful 3MF (your slice honored).
  AI pre-check + human review every order. White-label pack/ship, shipper of
  record, worldwide. Founding-seller discounts (60–90 days) live now.

## Integration (mirrors Prodigi pattern)

- Native **Etsy order sync live** (paid orders pull automatically); Shopify live;
  eBay/Amazon soon; public **/v1 API** + webhooks + CSV + manual entry.
- Upload STL/3MF/STEP/OBJ/ZIP → instant fulfilment + postage quote → list on Etsy
  at own retail → orders route → print/QA/pack/ship white-label + tracking writeback.
- Protected-design licensing: other sellers can list your design, you earn per-unit
  royalty, files stay locked to you.
