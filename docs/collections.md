# Collections, not catalogues (2026-09-25)

One universe, done obscenely well, beats twelve themes done thinly. Buyers join
a world, not a catalogue. Template: Bwizards. Brick only until the cult has members.

## Collection anatomy (Bwizards template)

- **Set props** (world furniture, built once): castle base, potion shelf tile,
  starfield backdrop, wand rack. Reused across every customer in the collection.
- **Couple template + AR** (flagship): two figures, kiss/bow pose, joint base,
  AR moment. The product the collection is famous for.
- **Pet examples**: familiar + owl + cat in wizard dress. Proves pets belong,
  seeds the add-on.
- **Solo + topper + ornament variants**: same avatars, context swaps (doctrine holds).
- **Add-a-member flow** ("complete your coven"): solo now, partner/pet/kid later
  at £24.99 a head. The collection grows with the customer — collectors complete
  things, shoppers compare things.

## Launch order (nine months, one asset library)

1. **Winter wizards** (Xmas/Q4 now).
2. **Same wizards, rose/red Valentine's skins** (January — palette swap, zero new assets).
3. **Birthday wizard** (hat + cake prop, evergreen behind both).

## Why cult beats catalogue

- One visual language across listings = instant brand recognition in search.
- Every new member-figure strengthens the set, not just the shelf.
- Marketing is one story ("join the Bwizards"), not twelve pitches.
- One quality bar learned deeply instead of twelve shallowly.
