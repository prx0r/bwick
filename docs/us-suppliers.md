# US suppliers — equivalents + complements (2026-09-25)

Why US fulfilment: most Etsy buyers are US-based. UK→US shipping costs margin
and days. Route US orders to US labs, UK/EU to Prodigi UK + Makr3D. No new
product work — same art, different lab.

## Flat print (Prodigi complements / backups)

Prodigi already prints in US labs via its global network — keep it as default.
Alternatives if a SKU is cheaper/faster stateside:

- **Printful** (350+ products, Etsy native, NA+EU fulfilment, no monthly fees,
  pay per order). Note: merging with Printify (verify live). Must disclose
  production partners in Etsy settings (Etsy rule).
- **Printify** (1,300+ products, 80+ providers, mockup generator, fee calculator).
  Widest catalogue; pick providers per SKU.
- **Gooten** (500+ lines, US independent, 40+ partners, USA/EU/UK/CA/MX). Fun SKUs
  relevant to us: gag/foil cards, premium puzzles, 2026 wall + desk calendars
  with templates, kiss-cut stickers, car magnets. Worth quoting against Prodigi
  per SKU — no monthly fees.

Rule: one lab per SKU per region (don't split one SKU across labs). Quote
Prodigi-US vs alternative per hero SKU, pick winner, lock it.

## 3D print fulfilment (Makr3D equivalents)

- **3D Vikings** (3dvikings.com) — closest US mirror of Makr3D: Etsy + Shopify
  integration, no monthly fees, no minimums, white-label pack/ship, tracking,
  money-back guarantee. Route US figure orders here; UK/EU stays Makr3D.
- **Printie** — Etsy-compatible 3D fulfilment partner model (blog documents the
  setup). Evaluate alongside 3D Vikings; validate with real test orders.

Decision: Makr3D (UK/EU) + 3D Vikings (US). Same STLs, region-routed at order
time. Validate both with a paid test figure before listing (quality + dispatch
time + packaging must match, or reviews split by region).

## Notes

- Etsy requires disclosing production partners (settings) — list Prodigi,
  Makr3D, and any US lab used. Transparency is a policy rule, not optional.
- Currency: price US listings in USD from US-lab costs; UK listings in GBP from
  UK-lab costs. Don't cross-subsidize shipping.
