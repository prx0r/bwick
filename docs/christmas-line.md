## Measure-and-react (see what sells)

Weekly review, every Monday Nov–Dec. Per SKU: units, revenue, reviews gained,
review rate (reviews/orders), return rate, question volume. Decisions:
- Top 3 velocity → double stock priority (Makr3D queue) + variant spin-offs.
- Zero sales after 50+ views → cut price 10% once, then kill and delist.
- High views + low conversion → fix photos/video first (traffic is fine, offer isn't).
- High returns/questions on one SKU → pause, fix, relist (protect Star Seller).
No opinions survive contact with the numbers. The line on Jan 1 is whatever sold.

## Why 30% works here

Volume × review velocity × Star Seller rank compounds harder than per-unit margin
at zero reviews. Mesh £0.13/pet amortized to noise within 3 orders. Premium
(£25–90) returns January on December's review base — discipline now buys pricing
power later. Never race below £8.99 on figures (perceived value floor) or £1.99
on paper (postage eats it).
