# Xmas stocking fillers + review engine (validated 2026-09-25)

Thesis: cheap cheerful SKUs aren't the profit center — they're the review engine.
Low price → high volume → many reviews → Star Seller + ranking → premium lines
(figures £25–40, memorial £60+) convert on that social proof. Every stocking
filler carries the QR video/AR moment competitors can't match, turning a £3
sticker into a 5-star photo review.

## Review engine mechanics

1. **Price for velocity**: stickers/tattoos/magnets at £2–4 are review machines
   (buyers review cheap delights fastest). Figures/fine goods ride their proof.
2. **Insert in every parcel** (Prodigi branding object — free): QR card → video/AR
   moment. Unexpected delight is what earns *photo* reviews, and photo reviews
   rank. Nobody else can attach a talking video to a sticker.
3. **Star Seller math**: 95%+ on-time ship + 5-star avg. Auto-fulfil (Prodigi +
   Makr3D) protects ship times structurally; the QR moment protects ratings.
4. **Follow-up**: post-delivery message at arrival estimate +3 days (Etsy-compliant,
   no incentives — the video already did the delighting).
5. **Q4 urgency**: list stocking SKUs by mid-Oct (indexing lead time); Xmas
   search peaks Nov; 2027 calendars ONLY sell Oct–Dec — miss it, wait a year.

## What NOT to do

Never pay/incentivize reviews (Etsy ban risk). Never fake reviews. Never ship
late in December (auto-fulfil + order by cut-off dates published in listings).
One 1-star "savage roast landed wrong" hurts more than ten 5-stars help —
reroll loop + safety line ship with V1 (see VISION).
