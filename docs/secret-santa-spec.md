## Friction autopsy (built into every listing)

1. **Price + date badges on thumbnails** ("£12.99 • arrives before the party") —
   kills price and timing questions before they're asked.
2. **Deadline calculator in listing** ("order by Dec 12 for party-day delivery").
   Immovable dates convert browsers; publish cut-offs 3×.
3. **"No photo? No problem"** — accept blurry phone pics, party candids, LinkedIn
   headshots. Floor-level upload bar + generic-funny fallback (no dead ends ever).
4. **Team-set listing (B2B Trojan horse)** — organizer buys 6–10 in one order
   (small bulk discount); six future customers in the room at opening. One
   transaction, office-wide acquisition.
5. **Gift-ready, zero wrapping** — box + ribbon + tag, stated 3×. Sell to buyer laziness.

## Comedy safety (office-safe by default)

Mild roast level on all office SKUs, never savage without explicit opt-in.
Reroll loop + safety line ship in V1. One wrong roast = 1-star spiral; the safety
line is review insurance, not garnish.

## Sequencing (locked)

NOW → Christmas: cheap, fast, funny, zero universes (Santa/elf/Rudolph = costumes,
not universes — same pipeline, holiday props). Reviews + ranking compound daily.
JANUARY: universes (wizard academy first) arrive to a trusting audience at premium
prices, converting cold traffic on the December review base. Discipline now buys
pricing power later.
