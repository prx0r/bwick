# PRODUCTS.md — cheap + fun, ~£10 anchor (v3)

Positioning: cheap and cheerful, not premium. Most things around £10.
Memorial line dropped (wrong emotion for the brand). Volume + reviews over margin.

Every product = theme + action(s) + video package, bound to fulfilment.
Costs: Meshy credits (verified), Makr3D GBP ex-VAT (verified examples),
Prodigi card ~£1.10 (verified; other Prodigi SKUs via quote endpoint —
dashboard/API key needed for exact figures, marked Q below).
Retail from validated Etsy bands. Margin = retail − fulfil − postage − fees (~15%).

## P1 — Roast Card + QR video (hero, live pipeline) [S tier]

- Theme `toon` + occasion skins (birthday, Xmas ×10 rendered).
- Action `take-turns` / solo roast. Video: 6s captioned teaser + full cut via QR.
- Prodigi Fine Art 5×7 (~£1.10) or Classic, Mail2Me/Mail4Me direct-to-recipient,
  Etsy auto-sync. Sticker insert in every parcel (branding object, free ride).
- Retail £4–6. Margin ~£2–3. List by mid-Oct (Q4 indexing lead time).

## P2 — Sticker pack + postcard + tattoos (attach) [S tier]

- Pet-face kiss-cut + transparent stickers, postcard variant of card art,
  **temporary tattoos** (kids go feral), **photo fridge magnets**.
- Prodigi stickers/postcards (pennies; exact via quote endpoint — Q).
- Purpose: margin + review velocity + bundle sweetener. Retail £2–4 / free over £X.

## P3 — Mini pet figure (premium upsell) [S tier]

- Theme `real` (multi-image mesh) or `brick` (6+30cr).
- Makr3D STL → figure. Single-colour PLA ≈ £1.29–2.50 ex-VAT fulfilment;
  multi/large US$7.60–10.30 — always quote the exact file first.
- **Start single-colour or two-tone max** (multi-colour purge waste needs human
  quote, can't auto-price). **EN71 toy-safety sits with us** (Makr3D doesn't
  certify) — position as collectible/keepsake, not toy, until certified.
- Retail £25–40 (validated $39–91 pet statues). Margin ~£15–25. No MOQ, 1–2 day.

## P4 — Couples / family set (basket doubler) [A tier]

- 2+ meshes, `kiss`/`hug`/`celebrate`, AR moment + video.
- Makr3D paired figures + Prodigi couple card. Retail £45–70
  (validated $44–45 couple, 8.1k sales). Phase 2: full crews (N meshes).

## P5 — dropped (memorial)

Cut per positioning: cheap+fun brand, wrong emotion. `sleep` action + sympathy
mechanics stay in the library for later; no memorial SKUs at launch.

## P6 — Jigsaw, calendar, photo book, framed print [A tier]

- **Jigsaw** (multi print areas incl. lid art): pet puzzle, proven gift category.
- **Calendar** ("12 months of Buster roasting you"): Q4 evergreen.
- **Photo book** (hardcover/layflat): "Year in Roasts" annual = retention hook.
- **Framed/canvas/metal prints + photo tiles**: wall-art money on fine-art stock.
- All Prodigi, same art, same order flow. Prices via quote endpoint (Q).

## P7 — Digital-only (pure margin) [S tier]

- Talking videos, AR moments (USDZ free with mesh), downloads, extra situations.
- Cost: compute only. Attach to every physical order + standalone (£3–8).

## P8 — Later / conditional [B tier]

- Phone/tablet cases, cushions, drinkware (avatar merch once a character has fans).
- Notebooks, wrapping paper, invitations (filler SKUs from same art).
- Apparel (weakest lane — Inkthreadable if ever, never launch with it).
- Brick sets, desk subscription (funded by P1–P4 revenue).

## Bundle economics (card + figurine, per order)

Mini-Buster single-colour (~80–100mm): fulfilment £2–4 → retail £24.99–29.99
as premium keepsake → ~£15–20 contribution after Etsy fees. Card attach adds
~£2–3. Per-order contribution £17–23 on ~£30 basket, zero inventory, 1–2 day
dispatch. (Model the full fee stack per SKU before listing — Etsy ~6.5% +
payment + $0.20 + possible 15% offsite ads.)
