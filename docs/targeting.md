# Targeting — buyer personas, keywords, angles (2026-09-25)

Checkout temperaments differ by persona: Sam + kids need speed/simplicity;
Chris needs approval loops + romance; Fiona needs hand-holding. One pipeline,
three temperaments. (Memorial dropped per brand positioning — no Margaret.)

## Lead vibes (crowned)

- **Wizard Xmas**: starry robes, glowing staff, snowy owl, ice base. Bridges
  wizard evergreen into Christmas, zero retooling. IP-safe (no names/marks).
- **Goth Xmas**: pumpkins, skeletons, black/orange, striped stockings in
  non-film patterns. Halloween-meets-Xmas aesthetic (predates any film).
  Zero competition in personalized goth-Christmas.

## Personas

**Secret-Santa Sam (coworker)** — buys mini figure, card, keychain (£8–15 cap).
Converts on: deadline guarantee, gift-ready, funny-not-savage (mild default).
Keywords: secret santa gifts, office christmas gift, funny stocking filler,
gifts under £15. Listing angle: price + date badge on thumbnail.

**Family Fiona (mom/grandma)** — buys family set, ornaments, stockings (£30–80).
Converts on: personalization proof (before/after photos), keepsake language,
"order by" dates, hand-holding copy. Keywords: personalized family christmas,
custom family ornament, grandma christmas gift, family keepsake.

**Coupled-up Chris (anniversary/Valentine)** — buys couple set, AR kiss, framed
print (£60–120). Converts on: romance, preview approval loop, gift box.
Keywords: couples christmas gift, our first christmas, personalized couple
figurine, anniversary keepsake. Angles: mistletoe AR moment (flagship),
his-and-hers roast cards (always sell the pair), matching ornament pair.

**Kid-adjacent (parents/aunts)** — buys chibi figure, stickers, tattoos (£10–25).
Converts on: cute, safe, fast. Keywords: kids christmas gift, stocking fillers
kids, chibi figure, christmas stickers kids.

## Quotes rule (structural advantage, say it in About)

Verbatim famous quotes = trademark minefield (rights-holder report pulls the
listing instantly, no court; repeat strikes suspend the shop; "everyone else
does it" means nothing against automated scanners). Our comedy is original
per-order — unreportable and uncopyable. Paraphrases don't save you if the
reference is obvious. Public domain (pre-1929: Dickens, Shakespeare, old
carols) is free — check USPTO for specific-use trademarks anyway.
