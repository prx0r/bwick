## The rule that governs everything

**Sell the feeling, not the franchise.** Aesthetic words are descriptive and
unprotectable (a COTTAGECORE trademark attempt failed at the USPTO). Franchise
names — in titles, **all13 tags**, descriptions, alt text — are trademark use
and the primary trigger for automated takedowns. "Inspired by", "unofficial",
tag-splitting: **no protection, flagged faster** (they read as evasion).

Custom requests ride the same gate already in canonical §3: `ip_check == pass`
required. Style/mood request → config, ship it. Franchise request → *"We cast
originals, not characters — tell us the feeling."*

## Safe vocabulary (write listings with these)

| pack | lead words | forbidden |
|---|---|---|
| Wizard/Fantasy | wizard school, magical academy, dark academia, spellbook, chosen one, cozy fantasy, dragon rider | any franchise school/boy/oracle names |
| Witch/Gothic | witchy aesthetic, cottage witch, gothic romance, moon phase, familiar | franchise witches, band/album palettes |
| Anime-Style | anime-style **original** character, chibi, magical girl, mecha pilot, shrine spirit, cel-shaded | every franchise name and fandom term (ARMY, BLINK…) |
| Xmas | first christmas couple, naughty list (generic), stocking stuffer, ornament | film-specific jumper/elf wording |
| Life-Events | nurse gift, teacher appreciation, graduation keepsake | logos, institutions |

Tarot: archetype names are public-domain symbolism; **no named deck art**
(canonical §7 already binds). Every pack ships with `ip_check: pass` only.

## What NOT to do (live examples)

- JuJuBit sells a *"Hogwarts"* style pack → one sweep from a rights holder
  away from a listing purge.
- MinfinityBricks built its catalogue on Spider-Man/Matrix/Maverick prints →
  a clock they don't control. Big sales, borrowed land.
- Our edge: the brick figure is **inherently original** (buyer's face + our
  costume packs + our props) — zero resemblance to anything licensed.
