# Simple products — who it's for (2026-09-25)

Five products. Every theme, season, and SKU plugs into one of them. If a new
idea doesn't fit these five, it doesn't ship.

1. **Dog** — the engine. Solo pup figures, ornaments, cards, stickers. Most orders.
2. **Cat** — same line, feline SKUs (elf ears fit already, librarian witch,
   angel). Second volume.
3. **Couple** — pair + AR kiss. Valentine's/anniversary/wedding + Xmas sets.
4. **Family (+ pet)** — crew that grows (add-a-member). Highest basket.
5. **Solo** — single human (self-gift, desk, grad, hobby). Wizard academy home.
