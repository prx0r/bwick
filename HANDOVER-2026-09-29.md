## Key Decisions Made Today

1. **PogPet is the wedge, pog.town is the platform** — Etsy validates, platform scales
2. **Cards use photos, not mesh renders** — flat 2D product, real photos work better
3. **Mesh is for 3D products** — figures, keychains, ornaments
4. **Store everything** — photo + mesh + renders = reusable assets forever
5. **H3 Max Turbo for product videos** — best quality for product shots
6. **8cm figures, 7cm ornaments** — optimal sizes for print and display
7. **PLA for all 3D products** — best detail, widest color choice
8. **Keychains are highest margin** — ~£1.50 cost, $12.99 retail
